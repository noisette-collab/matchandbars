Where to Watch Football in Amsterdam

Amsterdam has a lively sports-bar culture, especially concentrated around Leidseplein, Rembrandtplein and the historic city centre. On match days you’ll find both locals and international fans gathered in front of the screens.

The city is particularly strong for Premier League and Champions League coverage. Many bars also show Ajax matches when they play at home or in Europe. For NFL and rugby, the larger venues around Rembrandtplein and Rokin tend to be the most reliable.

Whether you’re visiting for a few days or living in the city, these places are the best starting point. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue before you go — TV rights and schedules can change.
