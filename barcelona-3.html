Where to Watch Football in Barcelona

Barcelona is Barça city. On match days the city turns blaugrana, and you’ll find both classic penyes and modern sports bars showing the game.

The most practical areas are the Eixample, the zone around Urquinaona / Plaça Catalunya, and the bars near Camp Nou. Eixample and the centre have the highest density of reliable Irish pubs and sports bars that show Champions League, Premier League and La Liga. Near Camp Nou you get pure Barça atmosphere on home match days.

The George Payne, Belushi’s, L’Ovella Negra and Michael Collins are among the most consistent places for big nights. Traditional penyes and neighbourhood bars deliver the most authentic local experience. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue.
