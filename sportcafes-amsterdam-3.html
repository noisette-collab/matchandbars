What is a Sportcafé?

A sportcafé is a typical Dutch café-bar where locals go to watch football. Unlike big international sports bars, sportcafés are usually smaller, more neighbourhood-oriented and deeply rooted in Dutch football culture — especially around Ajax and the Premier League.

The word comes from the Dutch tradition of combining a café (coffee, beer, snacks) with sports on TV. You’ll find them in almost every neighbourhood of Amsterdam, from De Pijp to Amsterdam West and the city centre.

Sportcafé vs Sports Bar

Sportcafés are smaller, more local and very Dutch. Sports bars (like Coco’s Outback or big international venues) are usually larger, with more screens and a more tourist-friendly atmosphere.

Both are great for watching football. If you want the local experience, go for a sportcafé.
