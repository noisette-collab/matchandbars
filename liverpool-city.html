Where to Watch Football in Liverpool

Liverpool is one of the most football-obsessed cities in the world. The best areas for reliable screens and atmosphere are the City Centre (around Concert Square and Mathew Street), the Baltic Triangle (especially Boxpark) and the historic Albert Dock.

On match days the city fills with red (and blue when Everton play). Dedicated sports bars and larger venues with many screens are the safest bets for Premier League, Champions League and big LFC nights. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue.

Whether you’re a local, visiting for a match or just want to catch the game with locals, these places are the best starting point.
