Where to Watch Football in Manchester

Manchester is a major football city with two huge clubs. The strongest concentration of dedicated sports bars is in Deansgate and the Northern Quarter.

Deansgate has large venues like BOX with dozens of screens and a proper matchday atmosphere. The Northern Quarter offers more characterful pubs such as Tib Street Tavern and Shack that fill up for big games. Near Old Trafford there are also classic pre-match pubs.

These places are reliable for Premier League, Champions League and big United (or City) nights. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue before you go.
