Where to Watch Football & Rugby in Dublin

Dublin is excellent for sport. The best concentration of screens is around O’Connell Street / O’Connell Bridge, St Stephen’s Green and Temple Bar.

Sinnotts, River Bar and Murray’s are reliable choices for Premier League, rugby and big matches.
