What is a peña?

A peña is an official Real Madrid supporters club. Fans meet at a fixed bar or venue to watch matches, travel to games and keep the madridista spirit alive. There are thousands of peñas in Spain and around the world.

Green cards with ✓ = peñas that are already part of the Match & Bars Instagram community.
