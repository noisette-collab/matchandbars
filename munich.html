Where to Watch Football & NFL in Munich

Munich is Bayern Munich territory. On match days the city fills with red, and the best places to watch are a mix of classic beer halls, Irish pubs and proper sports bars. The strongest concentration of screens is in Schwabing and the Altstadt.

Schwabing is the best neighbourhood for sports bars. It has a young, international crowd, multiple dedicated venues (Treffpunkt Sportsbar, The Keg Bar, Shamrock) and easy access to both the city centre and the English Garden. You can find locals, expats and visiting fans in the same room.

The Altstadt (around Marienplatz and Sendlinger Tor) is more touristy but excellent for big European nights and when you want atmosphere close to the main sights. Kennedy’s and Ned Kelly’s are reliable options there.

For pure Bundesliga and Bayern atmosphere, traditional beer halls and sports rooms attached to them still deliver. For Premier League, Champions League and NFL, the Irish pubs and modern sports bars in Schwabing are usually the safest bet. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue.
