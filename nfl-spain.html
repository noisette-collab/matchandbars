Where to Watch NFL in Spain

The best sports bars in Spain’s most visited cities to catch NFL games. Always confirm the fixture and screens with the venue.

Barcelona

The George Payne

Urquinaona · Plaça Urquinaona, 5

One of the most reliable spots in Barcelona for American sports. Multiple large screens, a solid selection of beers, and a lively atmosphere especially on Sunday afternoons. They regularly show NFL and are used to hosting fans.

Wild Rover

Raval · Carrer de Santa Mònica, 2

Irish-style pub with a good number of screens and a strong sports focus. Popular with both locals and tourists. NFL games are shown when there is demand, and the atmosphere is usually energetic.

Madrid

The James Joyce

Centro · Calle de Alcalá area

Classic sports bar in central Madrid with several screens. They show a wide range of international sports, including NFL. Good food and a comfortable setting for longer games.

Centro

Irish pub with multiple screens and a solid reputation for sports. NFL is regularly shown, especially big games. Lively crowd and good beer selection.
