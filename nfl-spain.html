Where to Watch NFL in Spain

Best sports bars in the most visited cities

History of NFL in Spain

1960s American football arrived in Spain through US military bases.
1990s The Barcelona Dragons played in the World League / NFL Europe.
1993 & 1994 The NFL staged two American Bowl pre-season games at Barcelona’s Olympic Stadium (49ers vs Steelers, and Broncos vs Raiders).
November 2025 First official regular-season game in Spain: Miami Dolphins vs Washington Commanders at the Santiago Bernabéu (78,610 spectators).
Since 2025 The NFL has signed a multi-year deal to return to Madrid.
