What is a penya?

A penya (plural: penyes) is an official supporters club of FC Barcelona. The word comes from Catalan and means a group of fans who meet regularly to support the team.

Penyes are recognised by the club. They organise match screenings, travel to games, run social activities and keep the Barça spirit alive far from Camp Nou. Most penyes have a fixed venue — usually a bar — where members and visitors can watch the matches together.

There are hundreds of penyes in Catalonia, Spain and around the world. On Match & Bars we list the ones that have a public meeting place so you can find them easily.
