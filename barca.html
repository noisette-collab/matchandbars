What is a penya?

A penya is an official FC Barcelona (Barça, Barca, FCB) supporters club. Fans meet at a fixed bar or venue to watch matches, travel to games and keep the Barça spirit alive. There are hundreds of penyes in Catalonia, Spain and around the world. Below are the ones currently listed on Match & Bars.
