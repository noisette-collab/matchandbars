Where to Watch Football & NFL in New York

New York has one of the best sports bar scenes in the world. Whether you want Premier League on Saturday morning, Champions League midweek or NFL on Sunday, you’ll find a place.

The strongest concentration is in Midtown (around 33rd Street and Penn Station) and the East Village. Midtown is practical if you’re near Madison Square Garden or the Empire State. The East Village has a more classic pub feel with loyal crowds.

Nevada Smith’s, Stout NYC and Legends are among the most reliable for both football and NFL. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue.
