Best Sports Bars in Madrid

Madrid is one of Europe’s great football cities. Whether you support Real Madrid, Atlético or just want to watch the Premier League and Champions League, there are plenty of reliable sports bars.

The best areas are Centro (Sol, Gran Vía, Alcalá) and around the Santiago Bernabéu. Irish pubs tend to show the biggest international matches, while traditional bars offer a more local atmosphere.
