Best Sports Bars in Paris

Paris has a solid selection of sports bars, especially popular with expats and tourists. The best areas for reliable screens and atmosphere are Saint-Germain, Latin Quarter and around Gare du Nord.

Whether you want to watch the Premier League, Champions League or PSG, these are some of the most consistent places in the city.
