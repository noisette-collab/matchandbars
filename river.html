What is a filial / peña?

A filial (or peña) is an official River Plate supporters club. Fans meet at a fixed bar or venue to watch matches, play football and keep the millonario spirit alive. There are active filiales in Barcelona, Madrid, Málaga, Valencia and cities in the US. Below are the main ones currently listed on Match & Bars.
