Best Sports Bars in London

London has one of the highest concentrations of sports bars in Europe. Because the city is so large and has so many clubs, the atmosphere and the type of fans you find can change noticeably from one area to another.

Central London (Soho, Covent Garden, Temple, Leicester Square) is the most international and neutral. Big multi-screen sports bars attract a mix of tourists, expats and fans of every club. These are usually the safest and most reliable places for any Premier League or Champions League match.

North London (Islington, Highbury, King’s Cross) leans heavily towards Arsenal. Many pubs around the Emirates show Arsenal games with a strong local crowd. King’s Cross itself is more mixed because of the station traffic.

West London (Chelsea, Fulham, Hammersmith) is Chelsea territory, with pockets of Fulham and QPR support. Stamford Bridge area pubs can be very Chelsea-oriented on match days.

East London (Stratford, Canary Wharf, Shoreditch) has a strong West Ham following, especially closer to the London Stadium. Canary Wharf is more corporate and international.

South London (Brixton, Clapham, Kennington, London Bridge) is more mixed, with Crystal Palace influence in the south. London Bridge has several large modern sports bars popular with a wide range of supporters.

For big neutral games or if you just want multiple screens and a guaranteed atmosphere, the central bars are usually the best choice. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue.
