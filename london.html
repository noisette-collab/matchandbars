Where to Watch Football in London

London has an enormous choice of sports bars. The best areas for a reliable screen and atmosphere are Soho / Temple, King’s Cross and parts of Canary Wharf.

Walkabout, The Sports Bar & Grill and Draft House are solid options. Always confirm the specific fixture.
