What makes an Official Manchester United Supporters Club (MUSC) special?

Manchester United has more than 350 official Supporters Clubs (MUSCs) in 94–95 countries — one of the largest networks in world football.

Distinctive features of official MUSCs:

  • Formal recognition by Manchester United FC.
  • Organised meetings to watch matches, travel to Old Trafford and connect with the club.
  • Access to exclusive events, pre-season tours and fan engagement programmes.
  • A global family that brings Reds together wherever they are.
