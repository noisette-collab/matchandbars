How Football Arrived and Grew in India

Football was introduced to India in the mid-19th century by British colonial officers and soldiers. The first recorded match took place in 1854, and by the late 1800s the game had spread among local communities, especially in Calcutta (now Kolkata), which quickly became the heart of Indian football.

After independence, Indian clubs and the national team enjoyed periods of success, particularly in the 1950s and 1960s. However, the real explosion of interest in European club football came in the 1990s and 2000s with the arrival of satellite television. Channels broadcasting the Premier League, La Liga and the Champions League turned Manchester United, Liverpool, Real Madrid and Barcelona into household names across the country.

Today, India has one of the largest audiences for European football outside Europe. Young fans in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Kolkata and other cities follow their clubs passionately, organise watch parties and form supporter groups that meet every weekend.
