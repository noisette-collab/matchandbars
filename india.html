European Football in India

Where to watch Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Bundesliga with local fans

Top Cities for European Football Fans in India

India has one of the fastest-growing audiences for European club football. These six cities concentrate the majority of supporters of Manchester United, Liverpool, Real Madrid, Barcelona, Arsenal, Chelsea and other big clubs.

1. Mumbai

Why it matters: Largest concentration of English Premier League fans in India. Strong communities for Manchester United, Liverpool, Chelsea and Arsenal. Many pubs and restaurants show matches on weekends and midweek.

Most followed teams: Manchester United, Liverpool, Chelsea, Arsenal, Real Madrid

2. Delhi NCR

Why it matters: High number of young fans. Strong presence of Arsenal, Manchester United and Real Madrid supporters. Several sports bars in South Delhi, Gurgaon and Noida.

Most followed teams: Arsenal, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Barcelona, Liverpool

3. Bangalore (Bengaluru)

Why it matters: Tech hub with a large young, English-speaking audience. Very active Liverpool and Manchester United communities. Good number of pubs that show Premier League and Champions League.

Most followed teams: Liverpool, Manchester United, Arsenal, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich

4. Kolkata

Why it matters: Historic football city. Surprisingly strong Barcelona following. Also significant Manchester United and Liverpool support. Several cafes and clubs show matches.

Most followed teams: Barcelona, Manchester United, Liverpool, Real Madrid

5. Hyderabad

Why it matters: Growing rapidly. Strong Manchester United and Real Madrid presence. Increasing number of sports bars showing European football.

Most followed teams: Manchester United, Real Madrid, Liverpool, Arsenal

6. Chennai

Why it matters: Solid Chelsea and Arsenal following. Growing interest in Premier League. Several venues in the city show big matches.

Most followed teams: Chelsea, Arsenal, Manchester United, Liverpool

Most Followed European Clubs in India

1. Manchester United

Still the biggest club in India by number of supporters. Strong presence in every major city.

2. Liverpool

Extremely popular among younger fans. Very active supporter groups in Mumbai, Bangalore and Delhi.

3. Real Madrid

Large following across the country, especially in Mumbai, Delhi and Hyderabad.

4. Barcelona

Particularly strong in Kolkata and Mumbai. Passionate fan clubs.

5. Arsenal

Consistent support, especially in Delhi, Bangalore and Chennai.

6. Chelsea

Strong in Mumbai and Chennai.

How to Watch Matches in India

  • Most big Premier League and Champions League matches are available on streaming platforms (JioCinema, Disney+ Hotstar, etc.).
  • Many sports bars and restaurants in the cities above show matches, especially on weekends.
  • Fan clubs often organise official or semi-official watch parties — follow their Instagram accounts.
  • For La Liga and Bundesliga, coverage varies; fan clubs are the best way to find where matches are shown.
