What is a supporters club?

An Arsenal supporters club (or branch of Arsenal America, etc.) is a group of Gooners who meet at a fixed bar or venue to watch matches, sing and keep the Arsenal spirit alive. There are hundreds of official and unofficial clubs worldwide. Below are some currently listed on Match & Bars.
