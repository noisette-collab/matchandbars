Where to Watch Football in Madrid

Madrid is one of Europe’s great football cities. Real Madrid dominate the conversation, but you’ll also find strong Atlético support and a big international crowd for Premier League and Champions League nights.

The best areas are the Centro (Sol, Gran Vía, Malasaña) and the zone around the Santiago Bernabéu. Centro has the highest concentration of Irish pubs and sports bars that reliably show multiple leagues. Near the Bernabéu you’ll find more pure madridista atmosphere, especially on match days.

Irish pubs such as La Fontana de Oro, Triskel Tavern and The Irish Rover are the safest bets for Premier League, Champions League and big La Liga nights. Traditional bars and peñas deliver the most authentic Real Madrid experience. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue.
