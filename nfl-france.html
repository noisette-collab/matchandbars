Where to Watch NFL in France

The best sports bars in France’s main cities to catch NFL games. Always confirm the fixture with the venue.

NFL in France — a short history

American football has a growing following in France, helped by the presence of French players in the NFL and by the league’s International Series games in Paris.

Paris has hosted several NFL International Series matches at the Stade de France and other venues, bringing regular-season football to French soil and boosting local interest among both expats and local fans.

When to watch NFL in France (regular season)

Most NFL games are shown in the evening and late night in France (CET). Typical weekly schedule:

  • Thursday Night Football — usually around 02:20 CET
  • Sunday early games — around 19:00 CET
  • Sunday late games — around 22:25 CET
  • Sunday Night Football — around 01:20 CET (Monday)
  • Monday Night Football — around 01:15 CET (Tuesday)

International games in Europe are usually played on Sunday afternoons local time, making them much easier to watch in France.

Where to watch NFL on TV in France

Main broadcasters for recent seasons include:

  • DAZN — significant portion of the regular season
  • beIN Sports — selected games
  • Canal+ — occasional coverage
