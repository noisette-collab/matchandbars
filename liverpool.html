What makes an Official Liverpool Supporters Club (OLSC) special?

There are more than 300 Official Liverpool Supporters Clubs (OLSCs) across around 100 countries. These are not informal groups — they are formally recognised by Liverpool FC.

The distinctive features of an OLSC include:

  • Official recognition by the club and access to club communications.
  • A fixed meeting venue where fans gather for every match, no matter the kick-off time.
  • Organised travel to Anfield, 5-a-side teams, and charitable work with the LFC Foundation.
  • A strong community spirit that represents the “Red Way” wherever they are in the world.
