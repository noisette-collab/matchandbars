What is an OLSC?

An Official Liverpool Supporters Club (OLSC) is recognised by Liverpool FC. Fans meet at a fixed bar or venue to watch matches, travel to Anfield and keep the Red spirit alive. There are more than 300 OLSCs worldwide. Below are some of the ones currently listed on Match & Bars.
