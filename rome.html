Where to Watch Football & NFL in Rome

Rome is a city of two football clubs (Roma and Lazio) and a growing number of international fans. The best places to watch matches are concentrated in the Centro Storico, with strong options also in Monti and Trastevere.

The Centro Storico (around Piazza Navona, Pantheon and Piazza Venezia) is the most practical neighbourhood for visitors. You get the highest density of proper sports bars and Irish pubs (Scholars Lounge, Highlander, La Botticella, Abbey Theatre) that show Serie A, Champions League, Premier League and NFL. The atmosphere mixes tourists, expats and local fans who prefer a neutral or well-equipped venue.

Monti is smaller and more local. Finnegan’s and Shamrock are solid choices there if you want a slightly less touristy feel while still having good screens. Trastevere is lively and beautiful but has fewer dedicated sports bars — better for atmosphere after the match than during it.

For pure Serie A passion you’ll also find bars near the Stadio Olimpico, but for convenience and reliable multi-sport coverage (including NFL), the Centro Storico is the clear winner. Always confirm the specific fixture with the venue.
