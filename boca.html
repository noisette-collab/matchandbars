Peñas, consulados and bars where you can watch every Boca match. The Superclásico has no borders.

What is a peña / consulado?

A peña (or Consulado outside Argentina) is an official Boca Juniors supporters club. Fans meet at a fixed bar or venue to watch matches, travel to games and keep the xeneize spirit alive. There are dozens of active consulados in Europe and the US. Below are the main ones currently listed on Match & Bars.
