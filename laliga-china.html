Where to Watch La Liga in China

After the Mbappé controversy: CCTV suspended, Migu continues

How to Watch Now Chinese Peñas

Current Situation (October 2026)

CCTV / Public TV — Suspended

China Media Group (CCTV) suspended all La Liga broadcasts on 26 October 2026 after Kylian Mbappé’s comments about a meme comparing him to Mao Zedong. Apologies from Mbappé, LaLiga and Real Madrid have been deemed insufficient so far.

Migu — Still Available

Migu (China Mobile) remains the main way to watch. It holds exclusive new-media rights until 2029/30 and streams all 380 La Liga matches per season on a freemium model (app + website). Chinese commentary and localization included.

Other platforms (iQIYI, etc.) may still carry selected matches. Check the Migu app for the most reliable coverage of Barça, Madrid, Clásicos and all other fixtures.

History of La Liga in China

2010s

Regular deals with CCTV. Spanish football very popular thanks to the big stars of Barcelona and Real Madrid.

2015–2020

CCTV renewals + streaming platforms (PPTV, iQIYI, LaLiga Plus DTC which later closed).

2021–2024

Migu becomes a major partner. After political tensions reduced free-to-air coverage, streaming filled the gap.

2024–2026

CMG/CCTV returns with non-exclusive rights (CCTV-5+). Migu signs exclusive new-media deal through 2029/30 (August 2026).

October 2026

CCTV suspends coverage over the Mbappé controversy. Migu continues as the primary platform.
