What is a peña?

A peña is an official Real Madrid supporters club. Fans meet at a fixed bar or venue to watch matches, travel to games and keep the madridista spirit alive. There are thousands of peñas in Spain and around the world. Below are some of the ones currently listed on Match & Bars.
